# Decisions and open questions

This review branch retains the endpoint-repaired CPU research reference, complete packet/GPU-range B1 horizons, saved native Flow* complete objects, selected cross-step-history and post-accept-refinement mechanism evidence, scoped numerical witnesses, and two paired CPU optimizations. The optional resident normal-composition prototype stays default-off with its local, B32×20 and continuous-prefix evidence. Complete lower/upper endpoint and tube x/y curves are the first comparison; a speed claim is stated only at the workload and sample count that produced it.

The evidence says local range and resident blocks are genuinely faster, but their online complete-prefix benefit is much smaller. Packet submission reductions gave only a small additional whole-prefix gain; the resident block won all five B32×20 pairs but missed the predeclared 1.20 paired-median target. Native Flow* still has a 44–356× same-workload wall-time scale advantage, with internal validation/history differences. These observations motivate examining a state-resident, whole-task architecture rather than another isolated small CUDA operator. They do not accept any existing external engine, prove the unique bottleneck, or define a paper contribution.

The separate `goal_whole_engine_feasibility_decision_20260915.md` is **deferred / not executed** in this branch. No Huan/Xiangru whole backend was newly adopted, repaired, benchmarked at long horizon or made default. Resident original-box T10/T20 remains not run. TORA/q3, DR7 fixed support, early three-tool Pareto, and withdrawn “we are fastest/tightest” statements remain historical, with fixed recovery/provenance; they are not home-page rankings.

Discussion with Xiangru and Huan can focus on three decisions:

1. Which error and acceptance contract should be the stable interface for a future whole-task backend, including retained coefficients, endpoint substitution and cross-step history ownership?
2. Is there a maintained complete device execution path or newer strict repair version whose source, tests and proof assumptions can be inspected without importing historical dirty results as current data?
3. For eventual NNCS use, which whole-task architecture has the best chance of closing the 44–356× observed engineering scale while preserving a reviewable numerical enclosure contract?
