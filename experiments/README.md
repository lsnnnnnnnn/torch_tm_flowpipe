# Experiment entrypoints

Active and supported:

- `consolidated_study/cli.py`: smoke and formal Torch TM / DiffReach / Flowstar
  pipeline.

The other experiment directories are historical or diagnostic support. Their
unique adapters and regression evidence remain available, but their runners,
collectors, reports, and committed result bundles are superseded. They are not
independent sources of benchmark truth and are not listed as supported
entrypoints.

Canonical configuration lives only in `../benchmarks/`.
